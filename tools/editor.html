PRISMHOOF COURSE EDITOR

Left-drag paints, right-drag erases, 1–8 pick a tile. Levels are 18 rows tall; the bottom row is the floor.

Test run

Runs the course on the spot, in the real debug build — same code path as a shipped level. Click the stage to give it the keyboard.

Reach guides

Hover the grid to measure from a tile. A jump rises 3 and carries 6; a pour reaches 10 from a standstill and 12 at a gallop; a pour into a jump climbs 5 over about 10. Gaps of 6–10 need a bridge, climbs of 4 need a ramp.
